# Changelog

## 1.0.0.5

### Added

- Random Bases (GC Factions Settings page): the map's airbases are dealt out to the factions at random, each base's neighbours and units going with it.

### Fixed

- The base deal waits until every base, carriers included, is in before dealing.

## 1.0.0.4

### Added

- Up to ten factions in one match, set with the Factions slider in Customize Mission or the mission editor.
- Every added faction has its own funds, warheads, supplies, colours and emblem, and shows on the faction select screen and the scoreboard. Each emblem is a flag in a broken ring, with the faction's symbol cut through it, and each faction's card on the select screen shows its own picture with the flag over it.
- A faction with no base is given a free one, and a map never gets more factions than it has bases, carriers included.
- The mission editor's base and unit faction lists offer every added faction.
- The faction settings tab lists every added faction, and its "Both" choice is now "All", so each faction's funds, warheads and supplies can be set on its own.
- The match goes on until one faction is left standing.
- The base game's AI of an added faction goes for the nearest base it does not hold, as a main faction's does for its mission objectives.
- The players of a knocked-out faction become spectators once they are out of the cockpit, and are told so in chat.
- A guest without the mod is told in chat that it only sees Boscali and Primeva, and where to get the mod.
- A GC Factions Settings page in Customize Mission with the faction count and a Unit Cap (the same setting as GC RTS's, shared between the factions; without GC RTS it holds the stock AI). With GC RTS installed its button sits beside GC's.
- Enemy factions show in their own colour in the kill feed, like on the map.
- The game's AI aircraft limit is shared between the factions, so a big multi-faction match stays playable.
